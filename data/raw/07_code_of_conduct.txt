MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — CODE OF CONDUCT
Document ID: HR-POL-001A | Version 7.0 | Effective: January 1, 2025

1. WORKPLACE BEHAVIOUR
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct. Harassment, discrimination, or
retaliation based on gender, religion, caste, disability, or sexual orientation is strictly
prohibited and grounds for immediate termination, independent of the standard PIP process
described in HR-POL-005.

2. PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT (POSH)
2.1 Meridian Retail maintains an Internal Committee (IC) as mandated by the POSH Act, 2013.
Complaints may be raised confidentially via posh@meridianretail.example or in person to any
IC member.
2.2 The IC is required to complete inquiry within 90 days of a complaint being filed.
Interim measures (transfer, leave to complainant) may be granted during the inquiry period
without prejudice to either party.

3. CONFLICT OF INTEREST
Employees must disclose any financial interest in vendors, suppliers, or competitors via
the annual COI declaration. Undisclosed conflicts discovered post-hoc are treated as a
policy violation regardless of intent.

4. GIFTS & ENTERTAINMENT FROM VENDORS
Gifts from vendors/suppliers above INR 2,000 in value must be declined or, if declining is
impractical (e.g. festival hampers), surrendered to Admin for the company gift pool.
Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (vouchers, gift cards) of any value must always be declined.

5. SOCIAL MEDIA CONDUCT
Employees must not disclose Confidential or Restricted information (per TECH-POL-002
Section 3) on personal social media. Employees identifying as Meridian Retail staff on
LinkedIn or similar platforms should include a standard disclaimer that views are personal.

6. DISCIPLINARY PROCESS
Minor violations result in a documented verbal warning, followed by a written warning for
repeat/second violations within 12 months. Major violations (harassment, fraud, security
breach, data theft) bypass the warning system and proceed directly to an inquiry committee,
which may recommend suspension or termination.
