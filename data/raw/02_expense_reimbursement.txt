MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Document ID: FIN-POL-014 | Version 2.1 | Effective: January 1, 2025

1. PURPOSE
Defines the process, limits, and approval hierarchy for reimbursement of business-related
expenses incurred by employees.

2. GENERAL CLAIM RULES
2.1 All expense claims must be submitted through the Concur portal within 30 days of the
expense date. Claims submitted after 30 days require Finance Head approval and will be
processed only in the next quarterly cycle.

2.2 Original invoices/bills are mandatory for claims above INR 500. For claims below INR
500, a self-declaration is sufficient, subject to a monthly cap of INR 2,000 in
self-declared claims per employee.

3. TRAVEL EXPENSES
3.1 Domestic air travel: Economy class only, for employees below Band 5. Band 5 and above
may book Premium Economy for flights exceeding 3 hours.
3.2 Local conveyance (cab/auto) for official work: reimbursed at actuals up to INR 800 per
day within city limits. Cab bookings must use the corporate Ola/Uber business account where
available.
3.3 Mileage for personal vehicle use on official duty: INR 8/km for two-wheelers, INR 12/km
for four-wheelers, capped at INR 1,500 per day.

4. LODGING
4.1 Domestic lodging is capped per city tier:
    Tier 1 cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune): INR 6,000/night
    Tier 2 cities: INR 4,000/night
    Tier 3 cities: INR 2,500/night
4.2 Lodging bookings must be made at least 3 working days in advance through the approved
travel desk unless the trip itself was approved with less than 3 days' notice.

5. MEALS & PER DIEM
5.1 Per diem for domestic travel: INR 1,200/day (covers all meals), disbursed in advance
upon trip approval and not requiring separate bill submission.
5.2 Client entertainment meals are reimbursed separately at actuals with prior manager
approval, subject to a cap of INR 3,000 per event for up to 4 attendees.

6. APPROVAL HIERARCHY
Claims up to INR 10,000: Reporting Manager approval only.
Claims INR 10,001 to INR 50,000: Reporting Manager + Department Head.
Claims above INR 50,000: Reporting Manager + Department Head + Finance Controller.
Note: this hierarchy applies to the cumulative monthly claim total per employee, not
per individual line item.

7. NON-REIMBURSABLE ITEMS
Alcohol (except pre-approved client entertainment), traffic fines, personal grooming,
in-flight upgrades, and mini-bar charges at hotels are not reimbursable under any
circumstances.
