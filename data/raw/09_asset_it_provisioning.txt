MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — IT ASSET PROVISIONING POLICY
Document ID: TECH-POL-007 | Version 1.8 | Effective: May 1, 2025

1. NEW HIRE PROVISIONING
Standard laptop provisioning (Band 1-5): Windows laptop, 16GB RAM, i5/Ryzen 5 equivalent,
provisioned 3 working days before joining date, subject to the hiring manager raising the
IT Request Portal ticket at least 7 working days in advance.
Band 6+ and Tech function employees: MacBook Pro or high-spec Windows workstation option,
32GB RAM minimum, provisioned via the same 7-day lead-time process.

2. ASSET REFRESH CYCLE
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years for standard roles, every 2 years for Tech/Engineering
roles given higher compute demands. Refresh requests before the cycle completes require
Department Head approval citing a specific business/performance justification.

3. MOBILE DEVICES
Company SIM cards with a monthly data/call allowance of INR 999 are issued to Band 4 and
above, and to any employee whose role requires field visits (Sales, Category Management,
Store Audit) regardless of band. Handsets are not provided by default; a handset allowance
of INR 20,000 (one-time, every 3 years) is available only to Band 6 and above.

4. LOANER EQUIPMENT
Loaner laptops are available for short business trips or when a primary device is under
repair, requestable via the IT Request Portal with 2 working days' notice. Loaner equipment
must be returned within 5 working days of the primary device becoming available again.

5. ASSET DE-PROVISIONING
On separation, per the Exit Policy (HR-POL-011 Section 4), all assets are collected by
IT/Admin and wiped per the data sanitization standard (NIST 800-88) before being
re-issued to another employee or retired.
