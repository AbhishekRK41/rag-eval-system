MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — LEAVE POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-001 | Version 4.2 | Effective: April 1, 2025

1. OVERVIEW
This policy governs all leave entitlements for employees of Meridian Retail Pvt Ltd across
its warehouse, corporate, and retail store functions. It supersedes HR-POL-001 v3.1.

2. LEAVE CATEGORIES
2.1 Earned Leave (EL): Employees accrue 1.5 days of Earned Leave per completed month of
service, totalling 18 days per calendar year. EL can be carried forward up to a maximum of
45 days. Unused EL beyond 45 days lapses at the end of the calendar year unless the employee
has applied for encashment before December 15.

2.2 Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per calendar year, credited in full at the start of the year.
CL cannot be carried forward and does not lapse into encashment. Maximum 3 consecutive CL
days can be taken without manager approval; beyond that, HR approval is mandatory.

2.3 Sick Leave (SL): 10 days per calendar year. Any SL exceeding 2 consecutive days requires
a medical certificate submitted within 3 working days of return.

2.4 Maternity Leave: 26 weeks as per the Maternity Benefit Act, applicable to female
employees with less than two surviving children. Employees with two or more children are
entitled to 12 weeks.

2.5 Paternity Leave: 15 calendar days, to be availed within 6 months of the child's birth.

3. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
Only Earned Leave is eligible for encashment. Encashment is processed once annually in the
January payroll cycle at the employee's basic salary rate. A maximum of 30 EL days can be
encashed in a single cycle. Employees resigning or being separated from the company will
have their full unused EL balance encashed as part of final settlement, subject to the
45-day cap described in Section 2.1.

4. LEAVE DURING PROBATION
Employees on probation (first 6 months) are entitled to CL and SL on a pro-rata basis but
cannot avail EL until confirmation. Probationary employees requiring extended leave beyond
their accrued CL/SL must apply for Leave Without Pay (LWP), subject to manager approval.

5. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY (LWP)
LWP may be granted for a maximum of 30 days in a calendar year. Any period of LWP exceeding
15 consecutive days will affect the employee's EL accrual for that month on a pro-rata basis.
LWP exceeding 60 days in aggregate over a rolling 12-month period requires review by the
Head of HR and may affect confirmation or promotion eligibility.

6. APPROVAL WORKFLOW
All leave (except emergency SL) must be applied through the HRMS portal at least 2 working
days in advance. Retail store staff must ensure store coverage is confirmed by their Store
Manager before leave is approved in the system.
