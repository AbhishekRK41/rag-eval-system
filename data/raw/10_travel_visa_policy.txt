MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL & VISA POLICY
Document ID: FIN-POL-019 | Version 1.4 | Effective: July 1, 2025

1. SCOPE
Applies to all employees undertaking company-sponsored international travel for supplier
visits, trade fairs, or training. This policy supplements, and where it conflicts
supersedes, the domestic provisions of the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-POL-014) for
international trips only.

2. CLASS OF TRAVEL
International flights under 6 hours: Economy. 6-9 hours: Premium Economy for Band 5+.
Above 9 hours: Business Class for Band 6+ only; Band 5 and below remain in Premium Economy
regardless of flight duration.

3. VISA & DOCUMENTATION
Visa fees, passport renewal (if expiring within 6 months of travel), and travel insurance
are fully reimbursed at actuals. Visa applications must be initiated at least 20 working
days before departure through the corporate travel desk; late applications requiring
expedited processing fees above INR 5,000 require Department Head approval.

4. PER DIEM (INTERNATIONAL)
Per diem rates are set quarterly based on destination cost-of-living bands (published
separately on the Finance intranet page) and disbursed in USD or local currency equivalent
before departure. Unlike domestic per diem (FIN-POL-014 Section 5.1), international per
diem requires submission of at least hotel and major meal receipts for reconciliation upon
return, even though it is disbursed as a fixed advance.

5. LODGING
International lodging caps are destination-band based rather than the domestic tier-city
system, published on the same Finance intranet page referenced in Section 4. Any booking
above the published cap requires Finance Controller approval prior to booking, not
after-the-fact.

6. TRAVEL INSURANCE & EMERGENCY
All international travelers are automatically enrolled in company group travel insurance
(medical + baggage + trip cancellation) at no personal cost. The 24x7 emergency assistance
number is provided in the travel itinerary email; employees must contact this number before
seeking non-emergency medical treatment abroad to ensure cashless processing.
