MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT & APPRAISAL POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-005 | Version 5.1 | Effective: April 1, 2025

1. APPRAISAL CYCLE
The performance year runs April 1 to March 31. Formal review touchpoints occur twice: a
Mid-Year Check-in (October) which is developmental and non-rated, and the Annual Appraisal
(April) which determines rating, increment, and bonus.

2. RATING SCALE
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: Outstanding (5), Exceeds Expectations (4), Meets
Expectations (3), Needs Improvement (2), Unsatisfactory (1). Ratings of 5 are capped at 10%
of any team's headcount (forced distribution) unless the Department Head obtains an
exception from the CHRO.

3. INCREMENT & BONUS LINKAGE
3.1 Annual increments are linked to rating: Rating 5 employees receive increments in the
top quartile of the approved budget band; Rating 1-2 employees typically receive no
increment and are placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) instead.
3.2 The variable bonus pool is a percentage of annual CTC ranging from 8% (Rating 3) to 20%
(Rating 5), pro-rated for employees who joined after October 1 of the performance year.

4. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN (PIP)
4.1 A PIP is a mandatory 60-90 day structured improvement window triggered by a Rating of
1 or 2, or by two consecutive quarters of missed KRAs regardless of annual rating.
4.2 PIP employees are reviewed every 3 weeks by their manager with HR present at the
midpoint and final review. Failure to meet PIP objectives at the final review may result in
separation, subject to Section 5 of the Employee Exit Policy (HR-POL-011).

5. PROBATION CONFIRMATION
Employees under probation are evaluated at the end of month 5 (not month 6) to allow time
for a confirmation decision before the 6-month mark. Confirmation requires a minimum rating
equivalent of "Meets Expectations." Extension of probation (max one extension of 3 months)
is permitted only with Department Head approval.

6. SELF-ASSESSMENT & 360 FEEDBACK
Self-assessment is mandatory for all employees ahead of the Annual Appraisal. 360-degree
feedback (minimum 3 peer inputs) is mandatory only for Band 6 and above (managerial roles).
