MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — IT SECURITY & ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Document ID: TECH-POL-002 | Version 6.0 | Effective: March 1, 2025

1. PASSWORD & AUTHENTICATION
1.1 All corporate accounts require multi-factor authentication (MFA). Passwords must be a
minimum of 12 characters, rotated every 90 days, and cannot reuse the last 5 passwords.
1.2 Privileged accounts (admin, database, production access) require rotation every 45 days
and mandatory hardware security key (YubiKey) MFA, not app-based OTP.

2. DEVICE MANAGEMENT
2.1 Only IT-issued laptops may access production systems and the internal HRMS/Finance
tools. BYOD (personal devices) may access email and calendar only, via the managed mobile
app, never through browser-based webmail.
2.2 Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT Security within 2 hours of discovery.
Failure to report within 24 hours may result in disciplinary action under the Code of
Conduct.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer PII and
payment data are always Restricted. Restricted data cannot be stored on local drives, USB
devices, or personal cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox) under any circumstances — only
on approved encrypted company systems.

4. VPN & REMOTE ACCESS
Remote access to internal systems requires the company VPN client at all times, including
for employees on the Hybrid/Remote policy (HR-POL-009). VPN sessions auto-terminate after
8 hours of inactivity and require re-authentication.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING
Suspected phishing, malware, or unauthorized access attempts must be reported to
security@meridianretail.example within 1 hour of discovery via the "Report Phishing" button
in Outlook, or directly if the button is unavailable. Do not forward suspicious emails to
colleagues.

6. THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE
Installation of any non-approved software, browser extensions, or SaaS tools requires
submission through the IT Request Portal and Security review before installation,
regardless of whether the tool is free or paid.
