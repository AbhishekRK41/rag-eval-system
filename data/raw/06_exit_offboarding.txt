MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — EMPLOYEE EXIT & OFFBOARDING POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-011 | Version 3.4 | Effective: February 1, 2025

1. NOTICE PERIOD
Standard notice period is 60 days for Band 4 and above, and 30 days for Band 1-3. Sales
and warehouse staff on the Operations track follow a 15-day notice period regardless of
band, given operational staffing needs.

2. NOTICE BUY-OUT
Employees may request to buy out part or all of their notice period by paying the
equivalent basic salary for the unserved period, subject to manager and HR approval. Buy-out
is not permitted for employees serving notice due to a PIP-related separation (see
HR-POL-005 Section 4).

3. FULL & FINAL SETTLEMENT (F&F)
3.1 F&F settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day, covering: pending
salary, unused EL encashment (per HR-POL-001 Section 3, capped at 45 days), pending expense
reimbursements, and gratuity if applicable (5+ years of continuous service).
3.2 Any outstanding loans, advances, or asset recovery dues are adjusted against the F&F
amount before disbursement.

4. ASSET RETURN
All company assets (laptop, ID card, access cards, SIM card, any peripherals) must be
returned to IT/Admin on or before the last working day. Delayed return beyond 7 days results
in the asset's depreciated value being deducted from F&F settlement.

5. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
A structured KT plan is mandatory for Band 4 and above, to be completed and signed off by
the reporting manager at least 5 working days before the last working day.

6. EXIT INTERVIEW
Conducted by HR within the final week, mandatory for all confirmed employees (not required
for probationary exits). Feedback is anonymized in aggregate reporting shared with
leadership quarterly.

7. REHIRE ELIGIBILITY
Employees who resign in good standing (non-PIP, completed notice or approved buy-out) are
eligible for rehire after a minimum gap of 6 months. Employees terminated for cause,
including policy violations under the IT Security Policy (TECH-POL-002) or Code of Conduct,
are not eligible for rehire.
