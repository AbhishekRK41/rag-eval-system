MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — REMOTE & HYBRID WORK POLICY
Document ID: HR-POL-009 | Version 3.0 | Effective: June 1, 2025

1. ELIGIBILITY
This policy applies only to Corporate function employees (Finance, HR, Tech, Marketing,
Category Management). Retail store and warehouse operations staff are not eligible for
remote work arrangements due to the nature of their roles.

2. HYBRID SCHEDULE
Corporate employees follow a 3+2 hybrid model: minimum 3 days in-office per week (Tuesday,
Wednesday, Thursday are mandatory anchor days), with Monday and Friday flexible for remote
work. Team leads may mandate additional in-office days during peak business periods
(festive season: September 15 - November 15, and end-of-quarter close weeks).

3. FULLY REMOTE EXCEPTIONS
Fully remote arrangements (5 days remote) may be approved by the Department Head + HR
Business Partner jointly, only for: (a) employees relocating for a spouse's job transfer,
(b) medical necessity supported by documentation, or (c) specialized tech roles explicitly
designated as remote-first by the CTO. Fully remote approvals are reviewed every 6 months.

4. WORK FROM HOME (OCCASIONAL)
Employees on the standard hybrid schedule may take up to 4 ad-hoc WFH days per month beyond
their flexible Monday/Friday, subject to manager approval via the HRMS portal, logged at
least 1 day in advance except in documented emergencies.

5. EQUIPMENT & REIMBURSEMENT
Employees working remotely are provided a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR
15,000 (laptop stand, chair, etc.), claimable once every 3 years. Internet reimbursement of
INR 1,000/month is available for employees on the Fully Remote arrangement only; standard
hybrid employees are not eligible for internet reimbursement.

6. PERFORMANCE & AVAILABILITY
All remote/hybrid employees must be available on core collaboration hours 10 AM - 4 PM IST
regardless of location, and must maintain calendar visibility. Manager discretion applies
for adjusting core hours for employees in different time zones (rare, requires CTO/CHRO
sign-off for cross-border remote work).
