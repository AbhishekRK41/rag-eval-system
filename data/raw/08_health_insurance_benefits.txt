MERIDIAN RETAIL PVT LTD — HEALTH INSURANCE & EMPLOYEE BENEFITS
Document ID: HR-POL-013 | Version 2.3 | Effective: April 1, 2025

1. GROUP MEDICLAIM INSURANCE (GMC)
1.1 All confirmed employees are covered under GMC from Day 1 of employment. Probationary
employees are also covered from Day 1 — coverage is not linked to confirmation status.
1.2 Base sum insured is INR 5,00,000 for the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent
children. Parents can be added as an optional rider at the employee's own cost via payroll
deduction.
1.3 Band 6 and above employees receive an enhanced sum insured of INR 10,00,000 and may add
parents at 50% company-subsidized cost.

2. CLAIM PROCESS
Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals (list on the insurer portal).
Reimbursement claims for non-network hospitals must be filed within 30 days of discharge
with original bills, discharge summary, and prescription records.

3. PERSONAL ACCIDENT & TERM LIFE INSURANCE
All employees are covered under Group Personal Accident insurance (3x annual CTC, capped at
INR 50,00,000) and Group Term Life insurance (2x annual CTC, capped at INR 30,00,000) at no
cost to the employee, effective from date of joining.

4. WELLNESS BENEFITS
4.1 Annual health check-up: employees and spouse eligible for a company-sponsored check-up
once per year at empanelled diagnostic centers, no separate claim needed (direct billing).
4.2 Gym/fitness reimbursement: INR 1,500/month, claimable quarterly, requires an active
membership receipt — this is a taxable perquisite and reflected in Form 16.

5. MATERNITY & FAMILY BENEFITS
Maternity-related hospitalization is covered up to INR 75,000 within the GMC sum insured
described above, in addition to Maternity Leave entitlements under HR-POL-001 Section 2.4.
Creche reimbursement of INR 5,000/month is available for children under 5 years, for
employees who have completed 1 year of service.

6. OPT-OUT
Employees with equivalent coverage through a spouse's employer may opt out of the dependent
addition (not the base employee cover, which is mandatory) by submitting proof of
alternate coverage during the annual benefits enrollment window in April.
